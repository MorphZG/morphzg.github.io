# Project: Personal Blog

## Purpose

Create a personal blog where the author can publish and maintain their writing. The site should make the writing the focus and give visitors a calm, welcoming place to read.

## Audience

Readers who have arrived to explore the author's writing. The specific subject matter and intended readership are not yet defined; keep the structure flexible until those choices are made.

## Experience and tone

The visual mood should feel mellow, cozy, and easy on the eyes. Use a restrained, harmonious color palette, clear typography, and generous breathing room. Keep navigation straightforward and make reading comfortable on both small and large screens. Avoid visual treatments that compete with the writing.

## Core needs

- Present recent writing clearly and make older posts easy to find.
- Give each post a comfortable, readable page with its title, publication date, and body.
- Support writing and publishing posts with Markdown or MDX.
- Provide clear site navigation and basic information about the author.
- Preserve useful sharing and discovery features such as page metadata and the existing RSS feed.

## Project foundations

- The site is built with Astro.
- Blog posts live in the `src/content/blog/` content collection and use Markdown or MDX.
- Keep the site lightweight and responsive; use JavaScript and dependencies only when they provide a clear benefit.
- Use semantic HTML, accessible contrast, visible keyboard focus, and descriptive link text.

## Success criteria

- A reader can reach a recent post from the home or blog page without confusion.
- Long-form text remains easy to read on mobile and desktop.
- The design feels calm and personal without reducing legibility or accessibility.
- The author can add a post using the established content format without changing site code.

## Open decisions

- Author name, site name, and domain.
- Main writing topics and intended audience.
- Whether the home page should feature a short introduction, a selected post, or only a post list.
- Whether posts need categories, tags, search, or an archive view beyond the current blog listing.
- Final typography, color palette, and any imagery or illustration style.
